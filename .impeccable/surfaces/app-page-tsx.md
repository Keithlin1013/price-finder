# Surface: Price Finder compare page (app/page.tsx)

Mode: Operate. The shopper enters a barcode or product name and needs the cheapest matching offer
per unit across Walmart, Target, Amazon and Costco, at a desk or one-handed on a phone in a store.
Bilingual copy, Chinese first with short English caps beneath. Chosen direction: Warehouse Sign
Card (roll). Memorable moment: the winning offer's unit price set as a warehouse aisle sign.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Offers are warehouse aisle sign cards clipped to an orange pallet-rack beam; the unit price is the headline. Refuses the category default of white result cards, store logos and a green "best price" badge.

OWN-WORLD: Cold concrete-grey floor (#c9cdcf family), off-white sign stock (#f7f8f6), ink #16181a, one rack-beam orange (#e8641b) for structure and the primary action, stamp red (#b3261e) only for "not a match". Heavy condensed grotesk numerals, clip tabs on sign tops, perforated-upright rhythm for the store column. Labels stack Chinese over spaced English caps (raise from Command Wall). Non-matches keep their card but carry a rotated 不同款 / NOT A MATCH stamp (raise from Ticket Wallet). Store states print as receipt-like lines, no badges or dots (raise from Phosphor Terminal).

STORY: Type what you are buying, press 比价. Four uprights print each store's progress. The rack fills: the cheapest-per-unit sign hangs first and largest; the rest line up by unit price; stamped non-matches sit below.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: an orange beam spanning the page holding the query field and the 比价 COMPARE button. Below it a four-column store strip (one line per store) that collapses to a 2x2 on phones. Then the winning sign: unit price at display scale, pack price and store beneath. Everything above the fold at 390px wide.

FORM: Warehouse Sign Card, position 3 of my ordered list; seed key 57de9666.
